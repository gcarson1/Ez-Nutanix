## Corrections to the brief

1. **"Prism Federation" is not a Nutanix product name.** Label it as multi-Prism-Central federation delivered by Nutanix Central (federated IAM, alerts, lifecycle, APIs, up to 25 Prism domains).
2. **Nutanix Central is no longer just SaaS.** It is SaaS (GA May 2024) and on-prem (GA Dec 2025), and as of 2026 it is the required host for NCM 2.x.
3. **NCM changed in 2026.** NCM 2.0 is decoupled from Prism Central, runs on Nutanix Central On-Prem, spans multiple PCs and vCenters, and **Cost Governance is now on-prem** (no separate Beam SaaS portal). Do not describe Cost Governance as a public-cloud SaaS tool.
4. **NCM tier mapping:** Self-Service and Cost Governance start at **Pro**, not Starter. Security Compliance (Security Central) and multi-VM app blueprints and approvals are **Ultimate** only.
5. **NKP Metal is not GA.** It was early access from Apr 2026 with a 2H 2026 GA target. NKP 2.19 was "available soon" as of Aug 26, 2026, and the latest posted release is 2.18. Do not say NKP Metal is shipping.
6. **Cloud Native AOS is GA only on Amazon EKS** (1.0.0, Jul 2025). Bare metal is still "future development" and tied to NKP Metal. AKS and GKE are not confirmed.
